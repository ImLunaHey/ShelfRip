Shelfrip is a free Windows app for your DVD, Blu-ray and 4K collection. It rips with MakeMKV, works out what's on the disc, and files the movie, every episode and the special features into Plex, Jellyfin, Emby or Kodi.

What it does

The slow part of ripping was never the rip. It's working out which title is the deleted scene and which is a trailer, then naming everything the way your server wants. Shelfrip does that part.

Names the extras

Special features are matched by run time against TheDiscDB, the shared Shelfrip disc database and (if you turn it on) DVDCompare, then sorted into Featurettes, Deleted Scenes, Behind the Scenes and more.

Works out the disc

Movie or TV, which film or show, the main feature, and which titles are episodes, in order. Menus, warnings, promos and Play All titles are set to Skip.

You check first

Nothing moves until you've seen the names on a review card. Change the match, rename an extra, Keep or Skip. Wrong later? Fix it on My shelf and the files move.

No quality lost

Every title is a remux: no re-encoding, every audio and subtitle track kept, commentaries named. Rip from several drives at once, or let auto-rip start when a disc goes in.

TV discs too

Episodes mastered as one long video are split by chapter. Discs you already have in the same or better quality are skipped.

Music CDs

CDs rip to FLAC with cover art and lyrics, named from MusicBrainz.

Your collection, on a shelf

Every disc you file lands on My shelf as a case, with format bands and box sets for seasons. Filing earns XP towards your next rank, with daily goals, streaks and badges.

Get started in a few minutes

  1. Install MakeMKV. It does the ripping, and is free while in beta.
  2. Download Shelfrip Setup and run it. No admin rights needed. It isn't code-signed yet, so Windows may ask you to click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Answer the setup questions: your media server, your library folder, a rip folder, and a free TMDB key.
  4. Put a disc in and click Rip. When it's done, check the names and click File.

Step-by-step help is in the README.

Better with every disc

With sharing on, every disc you file teaches the shared disc database what's on it, so the next person's rip of the same disc is named right first time. Only the disc's fingerprint, its title lengths and the names chosen are sent, never anything about you or your files, and the whole database is downloadable as CC0.
